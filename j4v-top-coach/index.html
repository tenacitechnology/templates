Chapter 01The Corpsman
1999 · Oakland, California

He started as the one they hand the wounded to.

Eighteen years old, sworn into the Navy as a hospital corpsman. The rating that means: when someone is hurt, you are the one they reach for. He learned how systems treat people at their worst moments, because he was standing there for twenty-five years of them.

Chapter 02Twenty-five years
25 years.

Three combat tours in Afghanistan. One in Iraq. Operations Enduring Freedom and Iraqi Freedom. He did not study this system from the outside. He served inside it.

Chapter 03Command Master Chief

The rank they come to.

Command Master Chief, the highest enlisted rank in the Navy. The person an entire crew goes to when it matters. Then a graduate degree in healthcare administration, because understanding people was never going to be enough. He had to understand the institution too.

That is the man who will sit across from you on the call.

Chapter 04The Holy Grail
